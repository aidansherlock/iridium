IRIDIUM

Everyone sees the odds. Almost nobody sees the price.

Iridium is research software for Kalshi, Polymarket and sportsbook prices. It works out what a contract is actually worth after fees, tells you plainly whether any edge is left, and keeps score on itself against the closing line. It never places a bet.

What it does

  1. Finds the fair price.It takes Pinnacle's line, the one most sharp bettors treat as the reference, and strips out the bookmaker's margin. What's left is a fair probability to compare every other price against.
  2. Subtracts the fees.Kalshi and Polymarket charge a fee on every trade, and it's biggest near 50¢. Iridium prices each market at the venues you use, after their fees, so the number you see is the number you'd actually get.
  3. Tells you if there is an edge.It shows the gap in plain English and a suggested stake. Every pick also becomes a paper order that's graded against the closing line, so you can see whether it was right without risking a dollar.
One pick, opened up: the price you can get, the fair probability, and the edge left after fees. Shown on demo data.
